    Catálogo alfabético de apellidos

    In 1849 Governor-General Narciso Clavería ordered that families without fixed surnames take one from this book, about 61,000 names, Spanish and native, sent to every province.

    This is a recreation, not a scan: every page is set in type from an open, machine-read transcription of the National Archives of the Philippines' 1973 reprint, read from two scans: Google's, of the University of Michigan's copy, and the Filipinas Heritage Library's. Each name keeps its column and line from the scanned page. Some readings are uncertain.

    How sure? shows how confident we are of each reading: faint names are our best guess, orange ones are blurry. Tap one to see the scan and tell us how you read it. Votes are anonymous: we keep the reading and a random code your browser makes up, nothing else.
